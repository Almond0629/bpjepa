# Large and restricted artifacts

Nothing here is in the repository. Checksums are SHA-256 of the files used for the paper. Acquisition names the source only; files derived from SoccerNet or DINOv3 are not redistributed here.

## Inputs you must obtain or generate

| Artifact | Purpose | Size (measured) | Source and terms |
|---|---|---:|---|
| SoccerNet-v2 videos and `Labels-v2.json` | everything | not measured | SoccerNet, under its NDA and terms; 300 train, 100 validation matches |
| 2 fps 224p frames, train | pretraining, evidence, 100F | 71 GB (3.3M JPEGs) | `third_party/eztorch_run/datasets/extract_soccernet.py` |
| 2 fps 224p frames, valid | evaluation | 24 GB | same |
| Label cache, train/valid | downstream | 227 MB / 76 MB | `precompute_soccernet_labels.py` |

Frame identity: the per-half measurement files record a SHA-256 for every JPEG. Row order: `configs/evidence/identities.json`.
